Our Story

Food is not just a meal —
it's an experience.

At Vrundavan Pure Veg, every thali begins with a promise passed down through generations of family kitchens: fresh ingredients, patient cooking, and a table where everyone — from grandparents to grandchildren — feels welcome.

Set in the heart of Nira, our restaurant blends the warmth of home-style Maharashtrian vegetarian cooking with the comfort and polish of premium hospitality — so your family occasions always feel a little special.
